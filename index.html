55+ Dual-Discipline Physio, Jet Lag & Packing

Key protocols for transitioning between indoor hardwood and beach sand, eastward jet lag recovery, and tournament packing.

✈️ Eastward Jet Lag Protocol (Canada ➔ Spain)

1
In-Flight Eastward Strategy:

On your overnight flight across the Atlantic, set your watch immediately to Spanish Time (CEST). Sleep as soon as meal service ends. Use eye masks and earplugs.

2
Day 1 in Benidorm (Sunlight Anchor):

Arrive in Benidorm, avoid taking a long 3-hour afternoon nap. Get 30+ minutes of natural Mediterranean sunlight on the promenade to reset your circadian clock.

3
Hydration & Electrolytes:

Airplane cabins cause extreme dehydration. Start electrolyte supplementation 24 hours prior to flight and maintain daily throughout October heat (24°C / 75°F).

🏐 55+ Dual-Discipline Joint & Recovery Strategy

🏖️
Sand Socks & Achilles Protection:

Deep sand dramatically strains calves, plantar fascia, and Achilles tendons. Wear specialized neoprene sand socks (Vincere / Tunturi) to prevent burns and support feet.

🧊
Cold Sea Water Hydrotherapy:

After heavy indoor matches on the Palau d'Esports hardwood, take a 10-minute cold wade in the Mediterranean Sea (Playa de Poniente) to reduce knee and ankle inflammation.

🩹
Rotator Cuff & Knee Warmup Routine:

Mandatory 15-minute resistance band warm-up before every match. Apply anti-inflammatory gel (Voltaren / Ibuprofen) post-play if managing joint stiffness.
